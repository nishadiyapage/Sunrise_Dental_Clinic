System User Guide & Help Center

Step-by-step instructions for staff and new users to navigate the system.

1. For New Staff & Administrators

  1. Log in using your assigned Admin Credentials on the Login page.
  2. Access the Admin Dashboard to view all current patient reservations in real-time.
  3. Verify patient appointment details including treatment type, assigned doctor, date, time, and calculated total bill.
  4. Manage schedules and ensure smooth operation during clinic hours.

2. For Patients: How to Book an Appointment

  1. Click Register in the top navigation bar to create a patient account.
  2. Log in using your registered Username & Password.
  3. Once logged in, navigate to the Book Appointment page.
  4. Fill in the required fields (Patient Name, Doctor Name, Treatment Type, Appointment Date & Time).
  5. Click Submit Appointment.
  6. View your Booking Confirmation card and click View Receipt to print or download your payment slip.
Important Rule: To ensure data security, only registered users who log in with a valid username and password can book an appointment.
